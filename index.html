About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Computer Science at Tsinghua University (advisor: Associate Prof. Youyou Lu). I reveived my Ph.D. degree at the Department of Computer Science, School of Informatics, Xiamen University (advisor: Prof. Jiwu Shu and Prof. Yiming Zhang).

My research centers on storage systems, with a particular emphasis on cloud storage and computing. My work has been published in top conferences like NSDI and FAST, and famous journals like TOS and TCAD.

Our research has also been adopted by both the open-source community and industry. In particular, our work, including PlanB, has been incorporated into OverlayBD, a prominent open-source project under the containerd organization. OverlayBD itself is deployed at scale by companies such as Alibaba Cloud, Azure, DeepSeek, and Kimi AgentENV (see its documentation).
